07 · About

I'm Chris. A freelance web and mobile developer and a final‑year Industrial Production Engineering student at FUTA. Engineering taught me to think in systems and tolerances, client work taught me to ship inside real deadlines and real budgets.

My core offering is native mobile development with React Native, Expo and Flutter, web builds on Wix, WordPress, GoDaddy or from scratch, and App Store and Play Store submission end to end. Base44 and Lovable come in early, for fast prototypes a client can react to before real production work starts, and Capacitor wraps a web app into native iOS and Android when that is the faster path. Outside the apps, I also build CRM and workflow automations and AI video ads, book trailers included. I run point on strategy and architecture and route overflow to a small team so delivery stays on schedule.
